# Sponsor word-master

English | [中文](./SPONSORING_CN.md)

word-master is a free, open-source Word document workflow: it turns a topic or source material into a natively editable DOCX whose styles come from a unit template contract, whose numbers come from fields, and whose captions number themselves. The project is independently maintained and always provider-neutral.

Sponsorship funds maintenance, compatibility work, template packages, examples, documentation, and infrastructure.

The project accepts sponsorship selectively. A partnership must genuinely help word-master users, and must never compromise the project's technical independence or user trust.

## Audience

- Readers work in engineering, design institutes, consulting, and public-sector document roles, plus developers who regularly produce technical reports, feasibility studies, and proposals.
- They use Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, pi, and other agent-capable tools daily.
- They care about one thing: whether the document survives a format review — not how flashy it looks.

## Minimum term

Corporate sponsorship runs for **at least one quarter (three consecutive months)** across all tiers above. Monthly prices are a unit of pricing only; single-month placements are not accepted.

Sponsor placements are long-term recommendations to users, not short-term ad slots. A partnership that appears for one month and disappears makes the sponsor block churn, and makes users doubt the credibility of everything displayed there — which is bad for the project and for the other partners.

Individual sponsorship is not subject to this minimum.

## Partner requirements

Partners should provide:

- An accurate product description and approved brand assets.
- A stable destination link, ideally with trackable referral parameters.
- A clear benefit for word-master users where applicable.
- A valid contact for operations or content updates.
- Timely notice of changes to pricing, availability, branding, or promotional terms.

All promotional claims are reviewed. Outdated, misleading, unavailable, or technically incompatible content may be corrected or removed.

## Project boundaries

Sponsorship does not include:

- Influence over the roadmap or the quality gate.
- Access to user files, prompts, generated documents, or usage data.
- Advertising inside documents exported by users.
- Auto-enabling a sponsor's service without the user choosing it.
- Custom development, private deployment, or a commercial support SLA.
- Endorsement beyond the agreed placement and copy.
